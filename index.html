A worked series by Arbaaz Khan

FEMM and Engineering Agents

From a first magnetic-field model to checking an AI agent’s experiment and reconstructing a published motor.

  1. Part 1: A First Electromagnetic Model in FEMM

    Build an air-core coil, understand the field equations, and calculate its inductance.

  2. Part 2: Giving an AI Agent a FEMM Experiment

    Use the coil from Part 1 to connect an agent’s prediction, tool call and numerical result.

  3. Part 3: Building a Second Brain for a FEMM Agent

    Follow one measurement method from a stored workflow to its commands, then check when that method stops applying.

  4. Part 4: Reproducing a Magnet-and-Coil Force Benchmark in FEMM

    Rebuild a small magnetic device, calculate the force on each part, and investigate an early result that missed the reference.

  5. Part 5: Reconstructing a Motor from a Journal Paper

    Follow one surface-magnet motor from its published dimensions to a flux-linkage match and an inductance discrepancy.
